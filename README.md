# 📊 Reliance Industries — Financial Analysis Model


> **A 6-sheet Investment Banking style financial model** analysing Reliance Industries Limited (NSE: RELIANCE) across FY2021-FY2025 — covering Income Statement, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, Ratio Analysis and an Executive Dashboard built entirely in Excel.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project replicates the financial modelling workflow used by Investment Banking analysts — sourcing real public data, structuring it into a clean Excel model, calculating key financial ratios and deriving actionable investment insights.

### Six Sheet Model Structure

| 📋 Cover | Company overview, model details |
| 📈 Income Statement | 5-year P&L with growth rates & CAGR |
| ⚖️ Balance Sheet | Assets, liabilities, equity & efficiency ratios |
| 💰 Cash Flow | Operating, investing & financing cash flows + FCF |
| 📊 Ratio Analysis | 20+ financial ratios across profitability, leverage & valuation |
| 🎯 Dashboard | Visual KPI summary with 4 Excel charts |

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

As Reliance Industries undergoes a structural transformation from a commodity-driven O2C business to high-margin consumer segments (Jio + Retail), this model answers:

- Is Reliance's revenue growth translating into profit growth?
- How is the company managing its debt and capital deployment?
- Are margins improving or compressing over 5 years?
- What is the intrinsic investment thesis for Reliance?

---

## 🔍 Key Findings

- 📈 **Revenue grew at 19.8% CAGR** — doubled from ₹4,66,307 Cr to ₹9,62,820 Cr in 4 years
- 💰 **FCF turned positive from FY2022** — signalling investment maturity after heavy Jio/Retail expansion
- 📊 **ROCE improved from 9% → 11%** despite ₹5L Cr+ capex — capital deployed productively
- 🔄 **OCF margin tripled** from 5.8% → 18.6% — core cash generation strengthening
- ⚠️ **Margin compression** — OPM volatile due to commodity price cycles in O2C segment
- 🎯 **Structural pivot underway** — O2C revenue share declining as Jio + Retail scale up

### Ratios Calculated (20+)
- Profitability: GPM, OPM, NPM, EBITDA Margin, ROE, ROCE, ROA
- Leverage: Debt/Equity, Debt/EBITDA, Interest Coverage
- Efficiency: Asset Turnover, Inventory Turnover, Debtor Days
- Valuation: P/E, P/B, EV/EBITDA, Market Cap to Sales

## 💡 Investment Thesis

Reliance Industries represents a unique convergence of energy, digital and consumer businesses. The company is transitioning from commodity-driven O2C to high-margin consumer segments while investing in New Energy for long-term growth. With improving FCF, rising ROCE and strong cash generation — Reliance is well positioned for sustainable value creation. Key risks include O2C margin volatility and rising debt from ongoing capex commitments.

---

## ⚠️ Risks Identified

- Margin compression from commodity price volatility in O2C segment
- Borrowings increased from ₹2,78,962 Cr → ₹3,74,313 Cr over 5 years
- Regulatory risk in Jio's telecom operations
- Execution risk in New Energy — early stage, unproven returns
